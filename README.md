A deadpan one-star review of an everyday essential, written for the
[One-Star Review of Something Essential](https://make.fun/c/u-0xb3e689-one-star-review-of-something-essential-c7234629)
competition on make.fun.

## The review

> Gravity never takes a day off, never gets thanked, and spends all of it dropping my phone face-down.

One star. Gravity works around the clock, takes no holidays, and devotes
every hour of it to dropping my phone face-down.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This validates the review (one line, at most 160 characters, non-empty) and
writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Candidates

Eight candidate reviews were drafted and scored against the judge's brief
(essential subject, sharpest true grievance, deadpan held, turn in the last
few words, tight and specific) in [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md). The
gravity review above scored highest and is the only entry submitted.
